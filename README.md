# Raj Shekhar Bose

I'm an environmental engineer and researcher at the Leibniz Institute for Agricultural Engineering and Bioeconomy (ATB Potsdam). I combine reactor experiments, microbial community analysis, and predictive modelling to study resource recovery from agricultural residues and wastewater.

## Research and methods

- **Anaerobic digestion and fermentation** — reactor stability, methane yield, and volatile fatty acid production.
- **Bioprocess modelling** — methane-yield forecasting, time-series validation, and model interpretation in Python.
- **Microbial ecology** — analysis of 16S rRNA amplicon data and microbial succession in engineered systems using QIIME 2 and R.
- **Bioprocess sustainability** — life cycle assessment and techno-economic analysis of resource recovery pathways.
